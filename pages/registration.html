Common Questions

When can I register?

Each student gets a registration appointment, which is the earliest time you can enroll. You can find it in your CSUN Portal checklist. After the appointment period ends, open registration lets any eligible student register.

What happens if a class is full?

You can join the wait list by checking the "Wait list if class is full" box when you enroll. If a seat opens and you meet all the requirements, you are added to the class automatically. You can wait list up to 13 units per term.

Why can't I register?

The most common reasons are a hold on your account, your appointment time has not started yet, or the class needs a permission number. Check your holds first.

What is a permission number and how do I get one?

A permission number lets you into a restricted class, for example one limited to a certain major. You get it from the department or instructor, then enter it when you enroll.

Can I change my schedule after classes start?

Yes. During the first week you can add, drop, or swap online. After that, adding usually needs instructor permission and drops have deadlines. See Late Schedule Changes in the official links below.

What if I got enrolled off the wait list?

Check your account balance right away. New fees may be due and missing the payment could drop you from your classes.

Registration Terms

Registration Appointment

Your assigned date and time when you can start enrolling.

Hold

A block on your account that stops you from registering until you clear it.

Shopping Cart

Where you place classes before you officially enroll in them.

Planner

A tool to save the classes you plan to take in future semesters.

Wait List

A line for a full class. You are added automatically if a seat opens.

Permission Number

A code that lets you into a restricted class.

Swap

Trade one class for another in one step without losing your first seat.

Prerequisite

A class you must pass before you can take another class.

Corequisite

A class you must take at the same time as another class.

Census Date

The day enrollment becomes official for the term, after which dropping is harder.

Unit Load

The total units you are enrolled in. During appointments the max is 18.

Open Registration

The period after appointments when any eligible student can register.
