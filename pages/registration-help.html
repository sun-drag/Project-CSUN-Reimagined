Registration Help Center

Guide to registering for classes at CSUN

Registration Steps

  1. Log into your student portal at my.csun.edu
  2. Navigate to "Course Registration"
  3. Search for courses by subject and course number
  4. Add courses to your shopping cart
  5. Review your schedule and finalize registration

Common Questions

When can I register? Registration dates vary by year level. Check your email for your specific registration window.

What if my course is full? You can add yourself to the waitlist or contact an academic advisor about alternatives.

Can I register after the deadline? Yes, but late registration fees may apply.
